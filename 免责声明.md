## 1. Purpose

"Book Learning Tutor" exists as an **Agent Skills open-standard skill** (installable into any compatible host such as WorkBuddy, Claude Code, Copilot CLI, Amp, OpenClaw). Its purpose is to help **the user themselves** "course-ify" their books (a **local file they supply**, or one **fetched from a public source under their explicit authorization**) and assist a human in learning chapter-by-chapter, lesson-by-lesson.

- We **provide no book content** and **provide no web content**.
- We **provide no website source code, book-source subscriptions, source rules, crawler programs, or proxy programs**.
- All raw files that get course-ified must be **supplied by the user** (a local file, or material lawfully saved locally).
